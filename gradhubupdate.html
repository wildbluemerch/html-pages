The Graduation Parade

7:30 – 10:30 AM · Wednesday, May 27

The day opens with cadets in full formation on Stillman Field — a tradition over six decades in the making. Bring your camera; this one's for the family album.

Location
Stillman Field
Attire
Spring or business casual. Comfortable walking shoes are mandatory. Dress in layers — Colorado mornings can be chilly.
Tips
Arrive early; seating fills fast. Bring clear water bottles, sunglasses, and sunscreen. Expect significant walking from parking to the field.

The Graduation Ceremony

9:00 AM – 1:00 PM · Thursday, May 28

The main event. Falcon Stadium fills with 30,000 family members, friends, and dignitaries. Caps fly. Thunderbirds roar overhead. Four years become a lifetime.

Location
Falcon Stadium
Attire
Layers are vital. Weather can shift from intense sun to rain or wind quickly. Comfortable shoes recommended.
Tips
Expect heavy traffic and arrive hours early — gates open at 6 AM. Follow the stadium packing list exactly to avoid being turned away.
Important — only clear bags are allowed for stadium entry.

Stadium Packing List

  • Clear bag (required)
  • Sunscreen
  • Chapstick
  • Hand fans (if hot)
  • Poncho
  • Mints
  • Snacks — trail mix (Permitted in one clear ziploc bag)
  • Sealed water bottle
