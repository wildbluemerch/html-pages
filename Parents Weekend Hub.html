Parking
Cadet Field House, Parade Field, and Cadet parking lots, with shuttle service. Arrive early — lots fill before the parade.
Attire
Business casual, or as specified per event. Bring layers and comfortable walking shoes — there's a lot of ground between venues.
Base Access
Every visitor must complete the Base Access form in advance. Bring a photo ID.
Tip — the Info Fair is the easy place to catch us. Grab your shirt Friday so you're set for Saturday's Whiteout.

Saturday, September 5

Tailgate in the morning, Whiteout at kickoff

Squadron tailgates fill the lots outside Falcon Stadium before the Air Force Falcons take on Duquesne. It's a designated Whiteout — wear white, and you'll be part of a stadium that looks like one big USAFA family.

Find Us
Look for the Wild Blue Merch setup at the east entrance of Lot 4, 8:00 – 10:00 AM. Come in white and ready for kickoff.
Stadium
Falcon Stadium follows a clear-bag policy. Check gate times on your ticket and plan for shuttle lines.
Weather
Full sun at altitude. Sunscreen, a hat, and water go a long way by the second quarter.

The USAFA Shirt

A new tradition — starting this Parents' Weekend

The front carries a clean AIR FORCE ACADEMY wordmark. The back tells the story: a hand-illustrated scene of cadets marching beneath the Cadet Chapel spires as jets streak overhead, framed by the words that define their four years — Integrity First • Service Before Self • Excellence in All We Do.

Printed on a Gildan 5000 heavyweight 100% US cotton tee. Classic relaxed fit, tear-away label. And yes — it's white, made for Saturday's Whiteout.

Every Shirt Gives Back

This is the tradition we're starting: every USAFA Shirt helps support the Academy community.

20% Of profits to Cadet Class Cabinets
